                Clearance

                Room the path planner keeps around the robot while driving: the envelope covers the robot in its driving pose (arms included), measured from the base frame. Saved to and applied to the running planner at once.

                Driving

                Speed limits for the path planner (capped by the robot profile) and how it drives. A stronger forward preference makes the robot turn to face along its path instead of moving sideways or backwards.

                Arm solutions

                An arm can usually reach a target in several ways. The robot prefers this elbow side, as seen from the robot (out = away from the body; the right arm is mirrored). A pose or point can set its own in the Teach tab. Saved to , used from the next arm move.

                Weights and joint-limit room

                How much each counts when solutions are compared. Joints closer to a limit than the room below cost more (an arm at a limit cannot move on, e.g. lift what it holds).
